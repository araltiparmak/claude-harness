Unpaid orders expire after 30 minutes

An order that is not paid within 30 minutes is cancelled and its stock goes back on sale. A payment that arrives after that is refunded automatically.

Summary

PM

Today an unpaid order holds its stock forever, so popular items show as sold out while nobody pays for them. With this change, stock is held for 30 minutes at most.

In scope

  • Cancel unpaid orders after 30 minutes
  • Release reserved stock on cancel
  • Refund payments that arrive late

Out of scope

  • Reminder emails before expiry
  • Different timeouts per product
  • Changes to the refund policy

Rules and examples

TestPM

Each example is a test case. Times are minutes after the order was placed.

R1An order waits 30 minutes for payment

The 30 minutes start when the order is saved, not when the customer opens the payment page.

IDGivenWhenThen
R1.1Order placed at 00:00Paid webhook at 29:59Order is PAID
R1.2Order placed at 00:00No payment by 30:00Order is CANCELLED within one minute (the job runs every minute)
R1.3Order CANCELLED at 30:00Paid webhook at 31:00Order stays CANCELLED, full refund is sent
R1.4Order placed at 00:00, still PENDING at 30:30 (the job has not run yet)Paid webhook at 30:30Order becomes CANCELLED, stock is released, full refund is sent

R2Stock is held only while an order is pending

Reserved stock goes back on sale the moment the order is cancelled.

IDGivenWhenThen
R2.11 item left, customer A has it in a PENDING orderCustomer B opens the productB sees "sold out"
R2.2Same as R2.1A's order expiresB can buy the item

R3A payment is processed once

Payment providers can send the same webhook more than once.

IDGivenWhenThen
R3.1Order PAIDThe same paid webhook arrives againNo second confirmation email, no state change
R3.2Order CANCELLEDThe same late webhook arrives twiceExactly one refund
